Inspect DAPI alignment locally

Load an H&E preview and its corresponding GigaTIME DAPI JPEG from the same field of view. Files stay in your browser. Move the slider to inspect whether bright DAPI structures align with the H&E nuclei. The two images are fitted to one physical field of view, so keep their origin and crop identical.

The overlap is a visual spatial check, not a protein validation result. Inspect several glandular, stromal and immune-rich fields before accepting the conversion.

Evaluation rules

Count unmatched detections. Match predicted to reference nuclei at IoU > 0.5 and report detection F1, class F1 and confusion by patient. Use separate patient-disjoint train/validation/test groups. Record image µm/pixel and model checkpoints. Do not infer cell type from GigaTIME scores and then reuse those inferred labels as ground truth.

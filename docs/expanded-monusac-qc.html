Additional MoNuSAC image QC

All 93 additional patient-separated tissue fields: scanner RGB, virtual DAPI, independent StarDist nuclei, virtual CK and virtual CD68.

Diagnostic images, not a new cell-type score. All 93 fields completed GigaTIME inference and were visually reviewed at contact-sheet scale. Only 9 passed the frozen DAPI nuclear-alignment and shadow gates; 84 failed. The failed fields are excluded from training and held-out accuracy. A DAPI pass alone does not validate all 23 virtual proteins, and the nine passing images still need marker-specific review before they contribute to a classifier.

The tissue came from scanner-RGB source images with recorded pixel spacing close to 0.25 µm/pixel; GigaTIME used a strict 0.25 µm/pixel inference grid. The five previews share source coordinates. Display scaling is for inspection only. A few fields may look plausible despite failing a prespecified boundary-contrast gate; changing that gate on these test images would bias evaluation.

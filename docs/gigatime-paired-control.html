GigaTIME paired positive control

Five author-supplied H&E/COMET test patches, scored with the pinned GigaTIME CNN on CPU against the authors’ cell-level binary marker targets.

Why this control matters. The authors train and test on marker-positive cell or nuclear regions, not raw fluorescence intensity. This page applies their mask-noncell and cell-label target construction to the published paired files, then compares all 23 model channels. AUROC measures localization of positive regions; average precision must be read alongside the positive-pixel prevalence.

Scope. These five patches come from the source-domain example archive and may share a specimen. They demonstrate that the checkpoint and input preprocessing can produce informative marker maps on paired reference data. They are not independent ORION or MoNuSAC validation, and favorable values here cannot justify cell typing on those datasets. TRITC and Cy5 are source-model background channels.

Across five patches

Rows with fewer than 50 positive or negative reference pixels are excluded from the per-channel AUROC/AP median. H&E was resized from 556 to 512 pixels; the CNN received four non-overlapping 256-pixel windows, matching the authors’ public test notebook. Each sheet shows H&E, authors’ binary target, virtual probability and red/green overlay.

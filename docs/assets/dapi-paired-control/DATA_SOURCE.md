# Paired GigaTIME control provenance

The displayed H&E field, thresholded fluorescence DAPI and nuclear outlines
come from the 50-patch sample evaluation archive linked by the official
[GigaTIME repository](https://github.com/prov-gigatime/GigaTIME#data).
The archive was downloaded from the repository's Dropbox link, with SHA-256
`cd1212b361f7bfe4f7f01779a59f437c691fd43a684d7b5f584c37bf7d0e2601`.
This page uses sample `0_24464_556_556`; the first five sorted H&E files were
previously used for exploratory calibration, and this is the first of the
remaining 45. The registered 556-pixel field spans 128 µm, according to the
[authors' scale clarification](https://github.com/prov-gigatime/GigaTIME/issues/4).

The virtual DAPI shown alongside it was generated by CellPhenotyper from the
pinned official CNN revision `a6556030bdb8071857559e12b921620aa1bbac5b`,
checkpoint SHA-256
`d200308302c7fefd13b259c62de546f2c859c28d1079e9f67dc8808915d95d0c`.
These thumbnails are provided for research evaluation and attribution, not
clinical use. Patient independence of the released patches is not established.

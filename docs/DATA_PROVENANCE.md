# GigaTIME training-data and Tambua evaluation audit

The public gallery contains **one ORION CRC33_01 patient**, shown at three locations within one calibrated window, and a separate completed **MoNuSAC lung-to-prostate transfer cohort** of 27 training ROIs from 9 patients and 10 test ROIs from 6 different patients. The three ORION fields must never be counted as three independent patients.

**Correction to the downstream evaluation design (19 October 2026).** The lung-to-prostate result is retained as an exploratory transfer experiment, not the requested same-tissue benchmark: its test organ was absent from fastPLS training. The replacement cohort is frozen from the original MoNuSAC organizer archives using a minimum image side of 512 native pixels and excluding any training patient present anywhere in the original test archive. It contains 88 training ROIs from 40 patients and 32 test ROIs from 19 different patients. Breast (27 train/8 test), kidney (15/10), lung (27/4), and prostate (19/10) each occur in both splits. Every expert cell type present in a given test tissue is represented in training images of that tissue. The corrected, completed conditional-classification test has 85.2% pooled accuracy and 42.1% four-class balanced accuracy over 10,776 cells; rare-class recall remains poor. No old score was transferred to this cohort.

The pinned gallery model is the original [GigaTIME CNN checkpoint](https://huggingface.co/prov-gigatime/GigaTIME/tree/a6556030bdb8071857559e12b921620aa1bbac5b), rather than GigaTIME-Flash. In the [GigaTIME paper, Method details](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Multimodal-AI-generates-virtual-population-for-tumor.pdf), its cross-modal translator was trained on **21 in-house paired H&E/mIF slides from 21 patients with lung adenocarcinoma**. The paper separately describes breast and brain tissues for generalization testing. The [official code](https://github.com/prov-gigatime/GigaTIME) describes application to TCGA slides; application is not translator training.

The original MoNuSAC test archive was inspected directly on Chiamaka. All **101** `.svs` headers agreed exactly with their paired XML `MicronsPerPixel` attributes; the recorded range was **0.2456–0.2527 µm/pixel**. The archive contains **25 patient folders**, and **32** ROI images span at least 512 pixels along both axes. This resolves the missing-spacing problem in the third-party mirror. The selected 27 lung training and 10 prostate test ROIs were verified to be patient-disjoint; the original full official train/test folders overlap at two patient IDs, so their nominal split alone is not sufficient. The companion annotations are XML polygon instances, organized by epithelial, lymphocyte, macrophage and neutrophil layers.

**Evaluation rule:** A test image must not be used to train or tune the model being scored. Freeze the GigaTIME checkpoint; fit Tambua's fastPLS classifier only on separate patients; reserve official MoNuSAC test patients for final scoring. Keep validation/model selection separate from the final test cohort. If any source overlap is discovered, remove the affected patients from the independent test set and disclose it. The ORION image is a technical demonstration, not an independent-patient benchmark. The primary MoNuSAC test is conditional on expert nuclei and shows 85.2% overall accuracy but only 1/46 macrophages and 0/4 neutrophils recovered. No MoNuSAC test image was used to fit fastPLS. Exact exclusion from the GigaTIME translator training set cannot be certified without its private slide manifest.

If the goal is **in-domain GigaTIME fine-tuning**, ORION supplies paired H&E and measured multiplex fluorescence, but it would require an ORION patient-level training/validation/test split; the same ORION patients cannot be used for both fitting and final evaluation. MoNuSAC supplies H&E images and expert nuclear instance/type annotations, **not paired multiplex fluorescence**, so it can evaluate the downstream Tambua cell-type pipeline but cannot directly supervise GigaTIME's H&E-to-mIF translator.

This audit concerns the **GigaTIME cross-modal translator**. It does not by itself establish the pretraining provenance of unrelated backbones or the CellViT/HoVer-Net checkpoints. Each comparator needs its own training-data and split audit before accuracy claims.

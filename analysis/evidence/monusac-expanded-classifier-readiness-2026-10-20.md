# Expanded MoNuSAC classifier readiness

The corrected independent CPU quantification bundle finished for all nine of the 93 added images that passed the frozen virtual-DAPI nuclear and shadow gates. The [corrected completion record](monusac-expanded-quantification-complete-v2-2026-10-20.json) gives two fitting, three development and four test fields. It conservatively matches only 8 fitting macrophages and 4 fitting neutrophils to independently detected StarDist nuclei. Twelve large macrophage source regions were flagged for review.

These are technical nuclear-QC passes, not whole-panel biological passes. The [first eight full-panel reviews](monusac-expanded-all-marker-review-2026-10-20/visual-review-all8.json) include distribution holds, one exclusion for 19 near-flat channels, and full-resolution review needs. The [ninth review](monusac-expanded-all-marker-review-2026-10-20/TCGA-YL-A9WL-01Z-00-DX1_2/visual-review.json) also holds its cell-type score pending measured-marker reference and full-resolution review. No added image has an accepted protein-specific marker validation. On an independent ORION CRC04 field, several of the ten registered raw measured/virtual marker pairs have weak or negative pixelwise association, even though DAPI and seam QC pass; see the [raw-image review](orion-raw-marker-visual-review-CRC04-2026-10-20/visual-review.json).

Accordingly, the added-cell fastPLS scorer requires an explicit `--exploratory` switch and labels its report as **not validated for publication as a biological cell-type score**. The nine-field exploratory viewer reports all calls and errors for diagnosis, rather than treating them as a validated benchmark. On four additional patient-held-out test fields, 184 conservatively matched detected nuclei yielded 66.3% pooled accuracy but only **44.6% four-class balanced accuracy**. Macrophage and neutrophil recall were both 0%. All 469 detected nuclei across the nine fields have displayed calls, including the unmatched nuclei whose correctness cannot be determined. The [visual review](monusac-expanded-exploratory-visual-review-2026-10-20/visual-review.json) flags broad DAPI and marker-specific concerns. The immediate needs are independent measured-marker validation, more QC-passing examples of both rare classes in fitting and testing, and patient-held-out evaluation after development decisions are frozen.

The first CPU quantification used an outdated CellPhenotyper copy that lacked virtual-score semantic fields and used the older mask-to-model-grid mapping. A second isolated CPU quantification used a pinned corrected quantifier with pixel-center mapping and the semantic contract. That exact quantifier (SHA-256 `84c2d4eb2addba7a8561372cb23b979f34f833e90dd0a5c036352ced30aec0d9`) was [merged into CellPhenotyper main in PR 8](https://github.com/tkcaccia/CellPhenotyper/pull/8) and verified in Chiamaka's updated checkout. Its optional `--exclude-mask` was not used in this MoNuSAC experiment. The value comparison across 21,574 per-cell channel means found a maximum absolute change of 0.01849 and mean absolute change of 0.00007264 on the [0, 1] scale; see the [quantifier comparison](monusac-expanded-exploratory-visual-review-2026-10-20/quantifier-comparison.json). The classifier uses only the corrected second quantification. The analysis wrapper now rejects quantifier tables that omit the semantic contract, so the stale handoff fails before fitting.

The prior corrected 120-image cohort remains a retrospective development evaluation. Its 94.26% matched-cell accuracy is not a four-class balanced score: it has no matched test neutrophils and misses both matched macrophages. The nine-image expansion does not resolve that limitation.

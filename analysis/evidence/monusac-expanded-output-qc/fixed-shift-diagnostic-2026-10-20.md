# DAPI spatial-shift diagnostic: do not deploy

The live scanner-RGB MoNuSAC expansion remained CPU-only and source-frozen. Its first 39 completed fields had one pass under the established independent StarDist-versus-virtual-DAPI QC. This diagnostic neither changed that QC nor edited any GigaTIME output.

On 22 completed **training** fields, [`audit_monusac_dapi_alignment.py`](../../audit_monusac_dapi_alignment.py) compared native virtual DAPI with a three-pixel Gaussian blur of independent StarDist masks over shifts within ±32 pixels. The median best shift was **−3 pixels vertically, +3 horizontally** on the 0.25 µm/pixel model grid; the median gain in DAPI-to-mask Pearson correlation was only **0.034**. The shift was selected without consulting test fields, but this exploratory selection still does not establish a physical registration error.

The frozen shift was then replayed on 39 completed fields with the original nuclear QC thresholds using [`audit_monusac_fixed_shift_qc.py`](../../audit_monusac_fixed_shift_qc.py). Whole-field recomputation of the *unshifted* AUC agreed with each original QC record within **0.00014**. Numeric QC passes changed from **1/39** to **9/39**, including four held-out test fields. These are **diagnostic pass counts, not validated additions**. Every shifted candidate was rendered with scanner RGB, original and shifted DAPI, a shifted-DAPI/StarDist overlay, CK and CD68 in the [first](fixed-shift-review-01.webp) and [second](fixed-shift-review-02.webp) contact sheets. The first four lung candidates have diffuse nuclear signal or strong virtual-DAPI signal on erythrocyte-rich structures; they were rejected by visual review. The other four have more plausible nuclear correspondence but remain held pending full-channel and independent-marker validation. No candidate entered fastPLS training or evaluation.

Crucially, [`audit_orion_dapi_pixel_alignment.py`](../../audit_orion_dapi_pixel_alignment.py) compared the same frozen MoNuSAC-derived shift against **raw measured Hoechst** and saved virtual DAPI on all ten reserved ORION patients. Source images were 2048 × 2048 at 0.325 µm/pixel and virtual DAPI was on the calibrated 0.25 µm/pixel grid; both were resampled to a common 1024 × 1024 physical field. The zero-shift spatial Pearson values ranged **0.628–0.805**. The fixed shift **reduced** correlation for **all ten** patients (median change **−0.0186**); local best offsets were zero for nine patients and one downsampled pixel for one. This argues against a universal CellPhenotyper/GigaTIME coordinate shift. ORION is a different source cohort and DAPI/Hoechst are related but nonidentical dyes, so it does not rule out MoNuSAC-specific registration or domain effects.

**Decision:** do not introduce a global shift into CellPhenotyper or count the shifted numeric passes as validated cells/images. Preserve the established QC exclusions. The original ORION and MoNuSAC outputs remain immutable. Investigate stain/domain effects with a positive control and independent measured markers before considering a dataset-specific correction. The 93-case CPU bundle continues; UCT HPC GPU work remains paused.

Evidence: [training-only shift search](all-completed-train-shift.json), [fixed-shift QC replay](fixed-shift-completed.json), [eight-image visual review manifest](fixed-shift-review-manifest.json), and [independently measured ORION comparison](orion-dapi-pixel-alignment.json).

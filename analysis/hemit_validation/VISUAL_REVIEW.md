# HEMIT paired-image review

The completed 287-image bundle was exported as one 1024-pixel-wide paired
contact per case. All 287 contacts were inspected in ten overview sheets,
30 cases per sheet at a 384-pixel contact width. No gross recurring square
border shadow or straight tile-seam pattern was apparent at this scale. This
is supported by the per-case technical seam QC: 287/287 pass. The overview
resolution cannot rule out subtler local artifacts.

Full-resolution contact images were inspected for cases 043 and 127 (highest
and lowest DAPI Spearman), 065 and 185 (lowest and highest CD3), and 151 and
126 (lowest and highest Pan-CK). These show DAPI generally follows nuclei,
though sparse tissue can have false bright virtual nuclei. CD3 and Pan-CK
often have misplaced or diffuse virtual signal relative to the same-section
measurement; case 065 has CD3 Spearman −0.332, and case 151 has Pan-CK
Spearman −0.249. Case 126 shows better Pan-CK architectural localization
(Spearman +0.690). The full 287-image viewer makes every comparison available
for further inspection without selecting only favorable examples.

The contacts use 256-pixel thumbnails of the original 1024 × 1024 patch in
each of four columns. Measured single-channel brightness is independently
scaled for display, while virtual probabilities are displayed on [0, 1].
Numerical scores use unmodified released target pixels and predicted
probabilities on source-H&E tissue pixels, not the contact image contrast.
No expert cell-type mask is supplied by HEMIT for this analysis.
